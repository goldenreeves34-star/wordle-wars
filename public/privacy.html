Wordle Wars privacy

Wordle Wars is a private family Wordle game run by Golden Reeves. Here's everything it keeps:

  • Your email address and name, so you can sign in (by an emailed code or with Google) and show up on the board.
  • Your games, scores and trash talk, so the board works.
  • A sign-in cookie on your phone, so you stay signed in.

Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads. To have your account and everything tied to it deleted, email golden@reevesrestorations.com.
